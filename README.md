# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Overview

This project analyzes over 1 million e-commerce transactions from 2009–2011 to identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, seasonal trends, and growth opportunities.

The analysis combines **Python/Pandas** for data cleaning and analysis with **Excel** for customer segmentation, PivotTables, and an executive dashboard.

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Excel / Google Sheets
- PivotTables
- RFM Analysis

## Analysis

The project covers:

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Revenue and seasonal trend analysis
- Geographic market analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Customer behavior analysis
- RFM customer segmentation
- Excel executive dashboard

## Key Insights

- The **top 20% of customers generate 77.2% of customer revenue**.
- **Q4 accounts for 36.9% of total revenue**, indicating strong seasonality.
- The **UK generates approximately 85% of total revenue**, showing significant geographic concentration.
- **Potential Loyalists represent 15.2% of customers** but average only 1.8 orders, indicating an opportunity to increase repeat purchases.
- Champion customers represent approximately **31% of customers but generate 75.9% of customer revenue**.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention of high-value customers through loyalty and personalized campaigns.
- Increase inventory and marketing activity ahead of the Q4 sales peak.
- Focus international growth on proven markets such as Ireland, the Netherlands, Germany, and France.
- Target Potential Loyalists with repeat-purchase incentives and personalized recommendations.
